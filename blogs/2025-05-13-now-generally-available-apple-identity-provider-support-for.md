<h2>Apple as a social identity provider, generally available!</h2>
<p>Hello everyone! I&#8217;m excited to announce that &#8220;Sign in with Apple&#8221; is now generally available for external tenants in Entra External ID. The feature has been in public preview since February, and many customers have already integrated Apple as an identity provider for their applications.</p>
<p>By configuring Apple as an external identity provider, you can add Apple as a social provider to your user flows. Then, users can sign up and sign in to the associated applications using their Apple ID accounts through a simple &#8220;Sign in with Apple&#8221; option.</p>
<p>An additional advantage is the ability to publish your social login-enabled mobile apps in the Apple App Store. As required by Apple’s <a href="https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/">App Store Review Guidelines</a> (section 4.8), if your applications offer third-party login options, it must include &#8220;Sign in with Apple&#8221; in addition to all your other identity providers. With Entra External ID, you can publish your Apple apps with social login capabilities.</p>
<h2>What&#8217;s next?</h2>
<p>We&#8217;re planning to extend Apple support to workforce tenants. We&#8217;ll also continue adding popular social identity providers as predefined options. While customers can use any identity provider that follows standard OpenID Connect and SAML/WSFed protocols, some providers use non-standard implementations. We&#8217;re actively working to support these providers as predefined options in our system.</p>
